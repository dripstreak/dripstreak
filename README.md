## Hello, I'm dripstreak 👋

Coffee, code, repeat.

I build web interfaces in TypeScript and React, mostly on Next.js, with Tailwind doing the styling and Vite when I need something small and fast. Most of my attention goes to the parts users never notice until they break: bundle size, render cost, and whether the whole thing still works from a keyboard. I care about design systems that hold up when twelve people are editing them, not just the ones that look good in a Figma file.

#### What I'm up to

- 🔭 Rolling out a shared component library and cutting our Next.js bundle sizes.
- 🤔 Thinking about design systems
- 🌍 Based in Singapore
- 🧩 I like small tools that do one thing well
